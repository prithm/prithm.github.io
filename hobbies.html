Hiking, travel & photography

Outside of shipping code, I'm usually on a trail, finding my way around a new city, or trying to get the shot right. A few things that keep the week-to-week interesting.

⛰️Hiking

Weekend trails are the reset button — nothing clears out a week of shipping code like a few hours of switchbacks and a view at the top. Always looking for the next trailhead, whichever coastline or mountain range it's near.

✈️Travel

New cities, new food, new ways people solve the same everyday problems — travel is the best way to keep perspective fresh when the day job narrows focus to one screen.

📷Amateur Photography

The camera comes along on every hike and trip. Mostly landscapes and mountains — still learning, but always chasing a better frame than the last one.
