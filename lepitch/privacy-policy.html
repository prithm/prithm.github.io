LePitch Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026 · Applies to the LePitch app for Android (package com.prithm.lepitch).

The short version:

  • LePitch has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads, and does not connect to the internet.
  • The only thing it sends anywhere is the short idea name you type, over Bluetooth, to phones physically near you that are also running LePitch.
  • Nothing is saved. When you stop, your idea expires, or you close the app, it is gone.
  • We, the developer, never receive, see, or store any of your data.

1. Who we are

LePitch is developed by Prithwish Mukherjee ("we", "us", "the developer"). This policy explains what information the LePitch app handles, why, and who can see it. If you have a question about it, see Contact us.

2. How LePitch works

LePitch lets people in the same room share a startup idea name with each other, phone to phone, using Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE). You can either "wave it out" (broadcast an idea name for 1, 5, or 10 minutes) or choose "just looking" (browse and like nearby ideas without broadcasting one). When someone likes an idea, both phones show the same color so the two people can find each other in the room.

All of this happens directly between nearby phones. There is no LePitch server, and the app does not request internet access.

What LePitch does not collect

LePitch does not collect, request, or have access to your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, location, advertising ID, device identifiers, or any account. It does not use cookies, analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or any third-party SDK that collects data.

4. Permissions and why we need them

  • Nearby devices / Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH_SCAN, BLUETOOTH_ADVERTISE, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT): to broadcast your idea name to nearby phones, find ideas around you, and send a like. On Android 12 and later, scanning is declared as not used to derive your location.
  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, Android 11 and earlier only): older versions of Android require this permission for any Bluetooth scan. LePitch does not read, use, or store your location.

You can revoke these permissions at any time in your phone's settings; LePitch will not be able to find or share ideas without them.

5. How information is used

Information is used only to make the app's single feature work: showing nearby ideas, letting you like one, and showing both people the same match color. It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any other purpose, and it is never sold.

6. Sharing

The only parties that receive any information are other LePitch users physically near you (roughly within Bluetooth range), as described in section 3. We do not share data with the developer, service providers, advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party, because the app never sends data anywhere except over Bluetooth to nearby phones.

We may disclose information only if required by law; however, because we never receive or store any user data, we have nothing to disclose.

7. Things to keep in mind about Bluetooth

  • Your idea name is public to the room. Anyone nearby running LePitch can see it while it is live. Bluetooth broadcasts are not encrypted, so someone nearby using other Bluetooth tools could also read it. Only type what you are comfortable sharing with people around you, and do not include personal or sensitive information.
  • Your phone is detectable nearby while broadcasting. Like any Bluetooth device, your phone sends a Bluetooth address while advertising. Android randomizes this address, and LePitch does not read or store it on other phones beyond the live connection.
  • "Just looking" does not broadcast. If you only browse, your phone does not advertise an idea and is not listed on other phones unless you like someone's idea.
  • Screenshots are blocked. LePitch prevents screenshots and screen recordings of its screens to help keep ideas from being copied.
  • We cannot control other devices. Once your idea name reaches another phone, the person holding that phone could note it down by other means.

8. Storage, retention, and deletion

LePitch does not save anything to your phone's storage or to any server. All information exists only in memory while a session is running. It is deleted when you tap stop, when your idea expires, when you like another idea (which withdraws your own), or when the app is closed. Since we never hold your data, there is nothing for us to delete on request, but you can always clear everything immediately by closing the app.

9. Security

Because LePitch keeps no data, has no accounts, and does not use the internet, there is no stored data that could be breached. Information sent over Bluetooth is limited to the items in section 3 and expires quickly. See section 7 for the limits of Bluetooth privacy.

10. Children

LePitch is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect information from children. The app does not collect personal information from anyone.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of your personal data. Because the developer does not collect or store any personal data, we hold nothing about you to provide, correct, or delete. You remain in full control: you choose whether to broadcast, what to type, and when to stop. If you have a question about your rights, contact us.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change how LePitch handles information, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches the app. If a change means the app would start collecting or sharing data in a new way, we will also tell you in the app.
